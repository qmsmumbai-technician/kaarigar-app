KAARIGAR

PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Who we are

Kaarigar is a home-services app that helps customers find nearby technicians (electricians, plumbers, painters and others) and call them directly. The app is operated by Abode Digital Technology LLP, Mumbai, India ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

Information we collect

If you are a technician:

  • Name and mobile number that you enter when you register.
  • Trades (services) you choose, up to two.
  • Your live location (GPS), only while you have tapped Go Online. The app sends your location about once a minute so nearby customers can see you.
  • Verification status and activity counts, such as the number of customer calls started from your card and your free-trial dates.
  • WhatsApp message: if you choose to verify your number, you send us a WhatsApp message from your own number. We use your WhatsApp number only to confirm it matches the number you registered.

If you are a customer:

  • Your approximate location is read on your device to show technicians near you and calculate distance. It is used on your device for this purpose and is not saved on our servers.
  • We do not ask customers for a name, phone number or email.

How we use it

  • To show customers which technicians are nearby and available, in the order of distance or rating.
  • To let a customer call a technician. The phone call is made through your phone's own dialer; we do not record or listen to calls.
  • To mark technicians as verified.
  • To run the free trial and the subscription for technicians.
  • To keep the service safe and fix problems.

We do not sell your information. We do not show advertisements. We do not use your information for advertising or sell it to data brokers.

What customers can see about a technician

When a technician is online, customers using the app can see the technician's name, verified status, trades, rating, approximate distance, and a button to call the technician's mobile number. The app shows customers the distance and does not display a map pin of the technician. To calculate that distance, the technician's latest saved location is part of the technician record that the app reads. After a technician goes offline or stops sending updates, he appears as not live and, after a while, is no longer shown.

Location permission

The app asks for location permission so it can work. Technicians' location is shared only while they are online. You can stop sharing at any time by tapping Go Offline, or by turning off location permission in your phone's settings. The current version shares location only while the app is open on your screen.

Where information is stored

Technician information is stored using Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), a service provided by Google. The app also loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your device may store a few small settings (such as your name, number and chosen role) locally so the app remembers you. Payments for subscriptions are made through your own UPI app; we do not see or store your bank or card details.

How long we keep it

Technician details are kept while the technician has an account. Live location is overwritten each time it updates; we do not keep a history of past locations. You can ask us to delete your account at any time (see below).

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Technicians can go offline at any time.
  • To delete your technician record and all information we hold about you, send a request from your registered mobile number or email to the contact below. We will delete it within 30 days.
  • You can also ask us to correct your information.

Children

Kaarigar is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the date at the top of this page.
